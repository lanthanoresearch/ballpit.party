⚪ BALL RUN

You're a ball. Everyone online shares one huge world and is trying to find the checkered flag. Nothing tells you where it is — or where anyone else is. Pick a direction and explore.

Ground — safe Ice — you slide Lava — back to spawn Water — hold jump to swim

A D / ← → move   W / ↑ / Space jump (hold = higher). Get the middle of your ball onto a block, or you'll roll off. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons.

A new map starts every 6 hours; you'll get warnings before it ends and everyone moves together. Open ⚙ to change your name, colours, zoom, bots and more.
