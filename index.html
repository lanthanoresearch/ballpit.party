BALLPIT.PARTY

You're a ball. Everyone online shares one huge world and is racing to find the checkered flag. Nothing tells you where it is, or where anyone else is. Pick a direction and explore.

Ground: safe to stand on Ice: slippery Lava: back to the start Water: hold jump to swim Yellow: push it! (only you feel it) Crown: someone finished

Move with AD or the arrows, jump with W or Space: tap for a hop, hold for a big jump. On a phone, use the on-screen controls (you can pick swipe styles in settings). Get the middle of your ball onto a block or you'll roll off.

The map changes every 6 hours and everyone moves together. Open the gear for your name, colours, zoom, bots and more.
