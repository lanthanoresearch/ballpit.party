BALLPIT.PARTY

You're a ball. In a public world, everyone online shares one huge world and is racing to find the checkered flag. Nothing tells you where it is, or where anyone else is. Pick a direction and explore.

Ground: safe to stand on Ice: slippery Lava: back to the start Water: hold jump to swim Yellow: push it! (only you feel it) Coins: just for fun Checkpoint flag: your next fall brings you back

Move with AD or the arrows, jump with W or Space: tap for a hop, hold for a big jump. On a phone, use the on-screen controls (you can pick swipe styles in settings). Get the middle of your ball onto a block or you'll roll off.

The map changes every 3 hours (12, 3, 6, 9 o'clock) and everyone moves together. Roll one way long enough and a ring fills above your ball: then boost! Jump three times in a row, each right as you land, and the ring fills. Then jump again for an extra tall jump. Touch a checkpoint flag and your next fall brings you back to it once. Tap the chat button to send an emoji or a quick message, and add friends in Settings to see where they are. Collect coins for fun, push things, bounce on pads, and look for the secrets. Open the gear for your name, colours, sounds, music, bots and more.
